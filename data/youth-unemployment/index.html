What the data tell us

Unemployment is only part of the problem: in Peru, many young people who do work hold informal, low-productivity jobs. This indicator is best read alongside youth informality and the share of young people not in employment, education or training.

For a fuller analysis, see the study Policies to improve youth labour market entry in Peru and the column Más oportunidades para los jóvenes (in Spanish).
